Input-Output Analysis for Just Transition: Linking Economic Structures and Social Inequality

分会场介绍：
The Input-Output (IO) Model is an effective tool for uncovering the direct and indirect impacts of economic activities across different industries and countries. This special session, titled ‚ÄúInput-Output Analysis for Just Transition: Linking Economic Structures and Social Inequality,‚Äù aims to use the IO model to explore inequalities in economic transitions and find potential solutions to address them, ultimately working towards achieving Sustainable Development Goal 10: ‚ÄúReduce inequality within and among countries‚Äù. The following themes will be covered in this special session:
Inequality in pollution and nexus impacts:
The IO model can combine environmental data with input-output tables to evaluate the environmental impacts, particularly pollution, of economic activities across industries and regions. This session invites IO research that explores the broader environmental impacts on areas like health burdens, productivity, and restoration costs, so as to provide scientific support for policy making.
Inequality in climate change mitigation and adaptation:
Economic activities contribute to climate change through greenhouse gas emissions, but the responsibility distribution shifts with the dynamics of global economic activities. Additionally, countries' vulnerability to climate change varies due to differences in economic structures. This session invites IO research exploring the complex interplay and inequality in economic and climate change mitigation and adaptation.
Economic structural changes and impacts just transition:
Economic development leads to structural changes, resulting in complex impacts on populations. While the income gap is one measure of economic inequality, there are additional indicators that reflect inequality within and between countries. This session welcomes research that innovates on the IO method, such as using network analysis and big data, to measure economic inequality amid changing economic structures.
This session aims to foster knowledge exchange on developing methods, databases, and research findings related to inequality in economic transitions using the IO model. By presenting selected high-quality research papers, the session will engage attendees with diverse research strengths and inspire cross-disciplinary ideas to address the challenge of a just economic transition.
