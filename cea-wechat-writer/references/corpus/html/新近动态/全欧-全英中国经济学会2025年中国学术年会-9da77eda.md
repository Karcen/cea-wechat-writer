Appendix: Background information about CEA and CEEP-BIT

The Chinese Economic Association (CEA) Europe/UK（全欧/全英中国经济学会）, established in 1988, is an independent, non-profit academic organization and one of Europe’s most influential bodies focused on China’s economic development. The CEA unites scholars, students, and business leaders who share an interest in China's economy, with the goal of deepening public understanding and promoting high-quality research in this field. Activities of the Association include the publication of a quarterly newsletter and occasional discussion papers, and the holding of an annual conference, seminars and workshops. CEA (UK/Europe) encourages research activity and scholarly exchanges between Britain, other European countries and China. (https://ceauk.org.uk)
BIT Center for Energy and Environmental Policy Research�(CEEP-BIT,�北京理工大学能源与环境政策研究中心) was established in 2009 with the approval of the University and originated from a research team specializing in complex resource and environmental systems, which was founded by Professor Yi-Ming Wei at the Chinese Academy of Sciences in the 1990s. CEEP-BIT focuses on the global scientific and technological frontiers and China’s major demands in the field of energy and climate change through scientific research and personnel training. It is committed to provide new discoveries about the interactions among energy system, climate system, carbon reduction system and socio-economic system, support scientific decision-making on energy transition and climate strategies and policies, and contribute to China’s modernization by training highly qualified interdisciplinary talents. (https://ceep.bit.edu.cn/english/index.htm)
