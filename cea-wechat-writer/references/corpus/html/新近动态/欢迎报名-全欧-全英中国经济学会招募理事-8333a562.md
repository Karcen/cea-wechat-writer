Call for Board Members of Chinese Economic Association (CEA, UK/Europe)

NEWS TODAY

全欧/全英中国经济学会（Chinese Economic Association UK/Europe，简称CEA）成立于1988年，是一个独立的非盈利的学术团体，是欧洲最具影响力的关注中国经济发展的学会。学会成员包括对中国经济发展感兴趣的学者、学生和企业高管。学会的宗旨是增进公众对中国经济发展的理解，并推动关于中国经济的高质量研究。学会出版英文期刊《Journal of Chinese Economic and Business Studies 》，其最新影响因子为2.4。现任主席为英国伦敦大学学院的米志付教授。

CEA学会诚邀具有学术领导力和奉献精神的优秀人士加入理事会，共同推动学会的持续发展和学术影响力。理事会成员将在学会的战略规划、重大决策和活动组织中发挥关键作用。

The Chinese Economic Association (CEA, UK/Europe), founded in 1988, is an independent, not-for-profit research association of scholars, researchers, students and business executives concerned with China's economic development. Its objectives are to advance the knowledge of the general public about economic development in China, and to promote and publish research on the Chinese economy. CEA publishes the Journal of Chinese Economic and Business Studies, which has an impact factor of 2.4. The current president of CEA is Professor Zhifu Mi from University College London.

CEA is now inviting outstanding individuals with academic leadership and dedication to join its Board to help further the association's development and academic influence. Board members will play a crucial role in the association's strategic planning, major decision-making, and event organization.
